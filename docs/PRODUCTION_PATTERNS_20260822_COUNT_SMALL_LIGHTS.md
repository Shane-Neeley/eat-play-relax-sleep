Date: 2026-08-22

This note records reusable, public-safe lessons from the independent EPRS Scheduler run. It is a method note, not a template for every release.

## What worked

- A gentle 7/8 grouped 2+2+3 pocket created a clear odd-meter identity without turning the song into a technical exercise. The missing final subdivision reads as a breath, and the changed bass orbit gives the complete return a different emotional angle.
- Four differentiated deterministic parts — muted pluck, rounded bass, soft rim, and glass pad — communicate a group-search premise without a lead vocal or external recording.
- The video translates the form directly with four luminous marbles on shallow curved basin paths. Removing one marble during the missing-count section and restoring it in a new orbit is immediately readable without text, faces, rails, tiles, or source footage.
- A bounded Sonic Pi cue/sync study helped test the 2+2+3 arrival while BeatScript remained the final portable renderer. Python/Pillow/FFmpeg kept the visual reproducible and rights-simple.
- The EPRS gates captured request, research, checksums, listening approvals, picture/video review, thumbnail evidence, publication handoff, and the external YouTube receipt in one lineage.

## What to carry forward carefully

- Keep the stable-floor plus one-structural-subtraction discipline, but leave 7/8, circular marbles, deep navy glow, and group-search metaphors behind on the next run.
- Preflight the visible Studio picker before final packaging. If the browser chooser reports `Not allowed` and the native picker reports `noWindowsAvailable`, stop external retries and use the authorized API fallback once rather than risking duplicate uploads.
- Telegram report delivery succeeded, but both WAV and video document sends timed out twice. Preserve the exact FINAL paths and errors, and test a small media handoff before the next final package.
